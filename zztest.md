## 答案与解析

**1.** • A selection pressure is an external agent that affects an organism's ability to survive and reproduce.
• Examples: predators, climate, food supply, disease, competition.  
　解析：选择压力＝影响生物能否在特定环境存活繁殖的外部因素。  
　出处：测试用例

**2.** m = (11 - 3) / (6 - 2) = 8 / 4 = 2. The gradient is 2, so for every 1 unit you move right the line rises 2 units.  
　解析：梯度＝纵向变化/横向变化。  
　出处：测试用例

**3.** • Large flat surface area to absorb light.
• Thin so gases diffuse in and out quickly.
• Many chloroplasts in the palisade layer for light absorption.
• Stomata and air spaces allow carbon dioxide in and oxygen out.  
　解析：叶片结构适应光合作用：面积、厚度、叶绿体、气孔。  
　出处：测试用例
