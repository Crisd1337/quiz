## 答案与解析

**1.** (a) 1 mark — Cordelia refuses to flatter or perform love for a reward; she will not compete with her sisters' speeches.
1 mark — her plain 'Nothing' signals honesty and a measured, private love (she loves 'according to my bond'), not an absence of feeling.
(b) 1 mark — Lear equates love with public declaration, so silence is read as rejection.
1 mark — his pun, 'Nothing will come of nothing', shows him thinking in terms of a bargain or debt rather than affection.
1 mark — he banishes Cordelia (and Kent) and divides the kingdom between Goneril and Regan, handing power to the daughters who will destroy him.  
　解析：两问是一组因果链：(a) 考 Cordelia 为什么说 Nothing（拒绝把爱当表演，爱是“本分”不是报价）；(b) 要串起三点——把言语当爱、用双关当交易、放逐造成权力真空。只写“他很生气”拿不到第 3 分。  
　出处：King Lear Act 1 Scene 1 (quote: Shakespeare, King Lear 1.1); Section 1 · King Lear short answer — exam format per LEEDER 10ENGB Homepage

**2.** (a) 1 mark — hyperbole and unverifiable comparison ('more than words can wield', 'dearer than eyesight, space and liberty').
1 mark — a catalogue of absolutes: love is measured against everything (sight, space, liberty, wealth) and therefore means nothing specific.
(b) 1 mark — the contrast with Cordelia's plain 'Nothing' exposes flattery as performance and truth as plain speech.
1 mark — it positions the audience above Lear: we can hear the inflation before he can (dramatic irony).
1 mark — it establishes the play's central error: love treated as language to be counted rather than felt.  
　解析：这题考“语言如何被量化”：Goneril 用夸张和绝对化清单，把爱变成可比较、可报价的东西。(b) 的重点是“对比 + 观众知道得比 Lear 多”，不要只写“她不是真心的”。  
　出处：King Lear Act 1 Scene 1 (quote: Shakespeare, King Lear 1.1); Section 1 · King Lear short answer — exam format per LEEDER 10ENGB Homepage

**3.** 1 mark — sight here means judgement or insight, not physical vision: Kent is telling Lear that he is misreading his daughters.
1 mark — Kent offers to be the 'blank' (the true target/centre) of Lear's eye, that is, the honest perspective Lear refuses.
1 mark — the image plants the blindness motif that structures the whole play: Gloucester is literally blinded, and Lear only learns to 'see' once he has lost everything.  
　解析：3 分分别给：sight = 判断力（不是视力）、Kent 自愿当“眼中靶心”的忠心、以及这一隐喻为全剧 blindness motif 埋线（后接 Gloucester 被挖眼、Lear 清醒时已一无所有）。  
　出处：King Lear Act 1 Scene 1 (quote: Shakespeare, King Lear 1.1); Section 1 · King Lear short answer — exam format per LEEDER 10ENGB Homepage

**4.** 1 mark — the dragon is Lear's self-image: power, terror and untouchability, a king who sees himself as beyond challenge.
1 mark — wrath has replaced judgement: he acts from injured pride rather than reason, and cannot tolerate being contradicted.
1 mark — because this flaw (not fate) drives the banishments and the division of the kingdom, his downfall follows from character, which is what makes the play a tragedy.  
　解析：hamartia 不是“倒霉”，而是性格缺陷：他把自己当成不可冒犯的“龙”，愤怒盖过判断，因此放逐与分国都是性格推动的结果——这正是悲剧结构要求的因果。  
　出处：King Lear Act 1 Scene 1 (quote: Shakespeare, King Lear 1.1); Section 1 · King Lear short answer — exam format per LEEDER 10ENGB Homepage

**5.** 1 mark — Lear still speaks as the giver: his identity is bound up in what he handed over, so he assumes authority continues after the gift.
1 mark — Regan's 'in good time' turns the gift into a completed transaction and cancels his claim on gratitude.
1 mark — the daughters now own the power he thought he kept; the title of king remains but the substance has gone.
1 mark — the play shows identity collapsing when power is transferred: Lear is reduced to 'a poor old man' and has to rebuild a self from bare humanity.  
　解析：4 分要覆盖三个概念：权力已转移、身份仍停留在“给予者”、以及作品借此说明“头衔≠实质”。Regan 那句冷冰冰的 'in good time' 是权力关系的转折点。  
　出处：King Lear Act 2 Scene 4 (quote: Shakespeare, King Lear 2.4); Section 1 · King Lear short answer — exam format per LEEDER 10ENGB Homepage

**6.** 1 mark — he argues that bare necessity is not enough for human dignity: even beggars have something 'superfluous' beyond survival.
1 mark — he is defending his hundred knights as a symbol of status and respect, not as a practical requirement.
1 mark — the speech shows him reasoning about humanity and hardship, but still from a king's position, so the insight is genuine yet incomplete.  
　解析：这题的关键是“need 有两层”：生存所需 vs 人的尊严所需。Lear 已经能替乞丐说话（这是进步），但他争夺的仍是排场与体面，说明此处认识尚未完成。  
　出处：King Lear Act 2 Scene 4 (quote: Shakespeare, King Lear 2.4); Section 1 · King Lear short answer — exam format per LEEDER 10ENGB Homepage

**7.** (a) 1 mark — the storm externalises Lear's inner rage and gives his fury a cosmic scale.
1 mark — it is also indifferent nature: wind and rain do not answer him, which cuts human authority down to size.
1 mark — by stripping him of shelter and followers it forces him to confront exposed humanity, preparing the 'poor naked wretches' speech.
(b) 1 mark — he sees himself chiefly as a victim: 'sinn'd against' comes first and carries the weight.
1 mark — he concedes only a lesser share of blame ('more ... than'), so the self-knowledge is partial, not full confession.
1 mark — fuller recognition comes later (Act 4, 'they told me I was every thing; 'tis a lie'), so this scene is a middle stage of his moral journey.  
　解析：(a) 暴风雨有三重功能：外化愤怒、显示自然的冷漠、剥掉权力外壳。(b) 注意 'more ... than' 的让步结构——他承认有错，但把自己主要写成受害者，所以要判“部分自觉”，不能说“他已经完全悔悟”。  
　出处：King Lear Act 3 Scene 2 (quote: Shakespeare, King Lear 3.2); Section 1 · King Lear short answer — exam format per LEEDER 10ENGB Homepage

**8.** 1 mark — for the first time he thinks about other people's suffering instead of listing his own injuries.
1 mark — he admits neglect as a ruler: 'too little care of this' is a confession of political and moral failure.
1 mark — the concrete imagery (houseless heads, unfed sides) makes poverty physical rather than abstract, so the audience feels it too.
1 mark — the insight only arrives after he loses power and shelter, so suffering becomes his teacher; the lesson costs him everything.  
　解析：4 分：①第一次想到别人的苦 ②承认统治者失责 ③具体意象让贫穷变成可感之物 ④这份觉悟是“失去权力”换来的。答题不要停在第①点，否则只拿 1 分。  
　出处：King Lear Act 3 Scene 4 (quote: Shakespeare, King Lear 3.4); Section 1 · King Lear short answer — exam format per LEEDER 10ENGB Homepage

**9.** 1 mark — 'lendings' treats clothes as borrowed status rather than as part of the self.
1 mark — stripping tests what a human being is when rank, retinue and clothing are removed.
1 mark — it dramatises the play's theme of identity: a king discovers he is only 'a poor, bare, forked animal', which is both a loss and a form of truth.  
　解析：3 分：衣服＝借来的身份、剥衣＝把人还原为“赤裸的人类”、以及这动作同时是失去也是觉悟。能点到 'poor, bare, forked animal' 的悖论更好（不必逐字引）。  
　出处：King Lear Act 3 Scene 4 (quote: Shakespeare, King Lear 3.4); Section 1 · King Lear short answer — exam format per LEEDER 10ENGB Homepage

**10.** 1 mark — the language ('vile jelly', 'lustre') turns eyes into objects, so the horror is deliberately grotesque rather than heroic.
1 mark — it literalises the blindness motif: Gloucester is punished in his eyes for the moral blindness that let Edmund deceive him.
1 mark — the staging is an abuse of power (a bound guest, a host's authority turned to torture), echoing Lear's abuse of Cordelia and Kent.
1 mark — the audience is forced to watch what the characters refused to see, so the scene makes sight a moral act for the viewer as well.  
　解析：这题要说明“字面失明 = 隐喻失明的兑现”：Gloucester 因看不清 Edmund 而失去眼睛；同时暴行是权力滥用，与 Lear 放逐亲人形成平行。最后一点答“观众被迫看见”可拿第 4 分。  
　出处：King Lear Act 3 Scene 7 (quote: Shakespeare, King Lear 3.7); Section 1 · King Lear short answer — exam format per LEEDER 10ENGB Homepage

**11.** 1 mark — when he had eyes he was morally blind, refusing to see Edmund's treachery and turning against Edgar.
1 mark — now that he is physically blind he sees his own errors and Edgar's love, so insight arrives after sight is gone.
1 mark — the paradox 'I stumbled when I saw' states the play's inversion: physical sight and moral vision are opposites, which also describes Lear's journey.  
　解析：3 分：①有眼时看不见真相 ②失明后才看清 ③点明全剧“视力/洞察”倒置的悖论。答题时最好把 Gloucester 与 Lear 的平行关系带一句，显示整体理解。  
　出处：King Lear Act 4 Scene 1 (quote: Shakespeare, King Lear 4.1); Section 1 · King Lear short answer — exam format per LEEDER 10ENGB Homepage

**12.** (a) 1 mark — he rejects the social and legal order (bastardy, custom, inheritance) and claims a 'natural' right instead.
1 mark — his goddess is appetite and self-interest, so 'Nature' becomes an excuse for predation rather than a moral authority.
(b) 1 mark — he is the engine of the plot: the forged letter sets Edgar running and destroys Gloucester's trust.
1 mark — his manipulation exposes other characters' blindness (Gloucester's credulity, Edgar's trust), which doubles Lear's error.
1 mark — his parallel family plot mirrors Lear's, so the two falls together argue that betrayal is a pattern, not an accident.  
　解析：(a) Edmund 把“自然”当女神，其实是把私欲合法化。(b) 三条功能：推动情节（伪造信件）、揭出他人的“看不见”、以及用平行情节强化主题。答到“恶的引擎”与“镜像结构”即可满分。  
　出处：King Lear Act 1 Scene 2 (quote: Shakespeare, King Lear 1.2); Section 1 · King Lear short answer — exam format per LEEDER 10ENGB Homepage

**13.** 1 mark — the audience watches Edmund stage both sides of the deception, so we know Edgar is innocent while Gloucester believes the letter.
1 mark — each character then acts on information the audience knows to be false, so their choices look like self-destruction.
1 mark — the gap between audience and characters turns the plot into a demonstration of how easily 'sight' (evidence, trust) can be manufactured.  
　解析：戏剧反讽的判分点：观众看到 Edmund 同时演两场戏 → 人物依据假信息行动 → 由此证明“看见/信任”是可以被伪造的。第 3 分要提升到主题层，不能只复述情节。  
　出处：Section 1 · King Lear short answer — exam format per LEEDER 10ENGB Homepage (Act 1 Scenes 1–3 questions and practice extracts)

**14.** 1 mark — the gods are 'wanton boys': careless, playful and not just, so divine authority is emptied of moral purpose.
1 mark — humans are flies: small, disposable and without control over their fate.
1 mark — the image reverses Gloucester's earlier confidence that the gods are just, and it answers Lear's repeated appeals to the heavens.
1 mark — the play leaves the question open: the innocent die (Cordelia) while the guilty partly escape, so the ending refuses to console the audience with divine justice.  
　解析：4 分：①神被写成顽童（不义） ②人是苍蝇（无掌控） ③这与 Gloucester 早先“神是公正的”信念相反，并回应 Lear 对诸神的呼喊 ④结局（Cordelia 之死）让“天理”问题悬空，突出悲剧性。  
　出处：King Lear Act 4 Scene 1 (quote: Shakespeare, King Lear 4.1); Section 1 · King Lear short answer — exam format per LEEDER 10ENGB Homepage

**15.** 1 mark — poetic justice would reward Cordelia's loyalty and punish the guilty in proportion, restoring a moral order.
1 mark — instead the innocent daughter is hanged on Edmund's order, and the reprieve arrives too late to save her.
1 mark — Lear's hard-won sight is rewarded only with her corpse: insight and loss arrive at the same moment.
1 mark — his final 'Look there, look there!' is ambiguous — he may die believing she lives — so the audience is denied a clear consolation.
1 mark — the deaths of Lear and Cordelia withhold the comfort of a restored order, which is why the ending feels unbearable rather than just.  
　解析：5 分依次是：诗性公正的定义 → 无辜者被处死且救援太迟 → 觉悟与失去同时发生 → 最后台词含糊、不给安慰 → 秩序未恢复，因此“不公”感是刻意的。答题时务必先给“什么是 poetic justice”，否则后面没有对照标准。

**17.** A. Both Lee and Parker show racism as a system that shields the guilty, but Lee builds a child's slow awareness while Parker forces the viewer to watch.

**18.** B. To state a comparative claim about both texts that answers part of the essay question

**22.** D. Lee controls what Scout notices and when she understands it, while Parker controls what the camera shows and when the viewer sees it.  
　解析：有效的比较要落到「作者如何控制信息与读者的反应」：Lee 用 Scout 的有限视角延迟理解，Parker 用取景与剪辑控制观众何时看见。「Lee's novel has chapters and a narrator, while Parker's film has scenes and actors who speak dialogue.」只是媒介形式差异，不构成比较点；「Lee's novel is set in the 1930s and Parker's film is set in the 1960s, so the film is more historically accurate than the novel.」把时代差异当成“更纪实”，没有依据；「Lee uses words to describe things and Parker uses pictures, which means the film is easier to understand.」是媒介常识加无根据判断。

**23.** A. Tom Robinson is convicted despite Atticus's defence, and the film's inquiry begins only because local police are implicated.

**24.** B. Both texts place responsibility on individuals who let prejudice pass unchallenged, not only on the law.  
　解析：个体责任＝普通人默许偏见也有责任，责任不止在制度。「Both texts place responsibility on the law alone, since individuals cannot be held accountable for prejudice.」把责任全推给法律，与主题相反；「Both texts place responsibility on families, since children learn prejudice at home and not in public.」把范围缩到家庭；「Both texts place responsibility on the main characters, since the wider community is never shown to be at fault.」说社群无责——三者都与两部作品的立场相反。
