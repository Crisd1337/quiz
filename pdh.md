## 答案与解析

**1.** Positive: trying a new sport such as rock climbing or martial arts; joining the school play or band; volunteering for a community cause or running for the school committee. Negative: binge drinking or vaping; sexting; getting a lift with a driver who has been drinking; dangerous driving; skipping school; trespassing or vandalism.  
　解析：得分点拆分：正面例子 3 个各 1 分（3 分）；负面例子 3 个各 1 分（3 分）。若再用一句话点出区别——正面风险仍带不确定性，但结果是新技能或成长；负面风险带来伤害、法律后果或健康损失——可另得 1 分。常见失分：把「坐酒驾者的车」当成驾驶行为本身，它同样属于负面风险。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Risk taking

**2.** Barriers: cost; fear about confidentiality; low health literacy (not realising a condition is treatable); transport and distance, especially in rural areas; long waiting times and complicated booking systems; language and communication difficulties; stigma and embarrassment; not knowing which service to contact. Effects: (1) Cost — a young person delays seeing a GP or dentist, so a treatable condition worsens and may eventually need hospital care. (2) Fear about confidentiality — they avoid discussing mental or sexual health with a doctor, so the problem goes untreated and can escalate into a crisis.  
　解析：得分点拆分：列出 5 个障碍各 1 分（5 分）；自选其中 2 个各解释健康影响 1 分（2 分），要写出「延误→恶化／并发症／危机」的因果链；表达与结构 1 分。常见失分：第二部分只是把障碍重说一遍（如「费用高所以很贵」），没有落到健康后果，不得分。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Barriers to accessing health services

**3.** A general practitioner (GP) under Medicare; Headspace or another youth mental health service; the school counsellor; a dental clinic; a sexual health clinic; physiotherapy; a community health centre; a pharmacy; a hospital emergency department; a dietitian or nutritional support service.  
　解析：得分点拆分：每写出一个具体服务 1 分，共 5 分。必须写具体机构或服务类型，只写「看医生」「去医院」这类笼统说法不给分；若能注明其中哪些对青少年免费或低价（Medicare、Headspace、学校辅导员）可加 1 分。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Health services for young people

**4.** A. Speeding – Fatigue – Drink/drug impairment – Not wearing a seatbelt – Distraction  
　解析：得分点拆分：排序全对 2 分，只对前半部分（超速第一、疲劳第二）1 分。2025 新州数据：超速第一（约 39–40% 致命撞车涉及超速，134 人死亡）；疲劳第二（约 20–21%，75 人死亡）；酒驾毒驾第三；未系安全带第四（29 人死亡）；分心第五（常被漏报）。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Rank the top 5 causes + Term 1 · Lesson 2–3 (2025 Fatal Five)

**5.** (a) Human factor. (b) Vehicle factor. (c) Environmental factor. The human category is easiest to change, because the driver directly controls their own behaviour — speed, phone use, seatbelt use, fatigue and alcohol can all be changed through education, laws and police enforcement. Vehicle and environmental factors also matter, but about 95% of crashes involve human factors alone or in combination, so changing driver behaviour has the biggest effect on the road toll.  
　解析：得分点拆分：三项分类各 1 分（3 分）；指出人为因素最易改变 1 分；理由 2 分（驾驶人可控、约 95% 事故涉及人为因素、靠教育＋立法＋执法改变）。常见失分：只写「人为因素最多」而没解释「为什么可以被改变」，理由分只能拿一半。  
　出处：Term 1 · Lesson 2–3 Factors influencing road and traffic incidents（对应清单 List the major causes 一题）

**6.** B. Using a mobile phone and interacting with passengers  
　解析：得分点拆分：认出两项各 1 分（2 分）。手机与乘客互动是最主要的两项——用手机时撞车风险约提高 4 倍；P 牌司机载 3 名以上乘客事故几率翻倍以上。其余选项（音响、吃喝、导航）确实属于车内分心，但不是最常见的两项，选它们会失去「最常见」的分数。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Driver distractions + Term 1 · Lesson 2–3 & Lesson 5 Safety Plans

**7.** C. 0.00 for learner, 0.00 for provisional, 0.05 for full  
　解析：得分点拆分：三个空各 1 分——Learner 0.00、Provisional 0.00、Full 0.05。最常见的错法是把 P 牌写成 0.05；新州 P 牌与 L 牌一样必须为零，全牌上限才是 0.05。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · BAC（数值按 NSW 法定标准，课程标准补充）

**8.** D. Social, pleasure (intermittent reward) and pain  
　解析：得分点拆分：三个空各 1 分（3 分）——Social（如 Farmville，和朋友一起经营自己的世界）、Pleasure／intermittent reward（如 Call of Duty，间歇给奖励，让人一直想再来一次）、Pain（如 World of Warcraft，用掉段位、清空奖励惩罚下线的人）。只写「好玩」「上瘾」这类笼统词不给分。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Games – three motivational drives + Term 1 · Lesson 9 Online Safety

**9.** Mutual respect; trust; honesty; open communication; consent; equality (no one holds all the power); freedom to be yourself; support and encouragement.  
　解析：得分点拆分：每个特征 1 分，共 5 分。要写课程术语（mutual respect / trust / consent / open communication / equality），只写「对你好」「不吵架」这类笼统描述不给分；写出 6 个但有一个重复，仍只按 5 个计。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Features of respectful relationships

**10.** Using influence to support and encourage the other person; making decisions together and giving them a voice; standing up for someone who is being bullied; mentoring a younger player and sharing responsibility; being honest about your own limits and asking for their input; using a leadership role to make sure everyone is included.  
　解析：得分点拆分：每个具体例子 1 分，通常给 3–4 分。判断标准是「权力被用来让对方更有选择、让关系更平等」。注意：替对方做所有决定即使出发点是保护，也属于负面（控制）用法，这一点常被写反。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Positive use of power

**11.** Coercion or threats; manipulation and guilt-tripping; peer pressure to do something risky; bullying or public humiliation; controlling who the other person sees or what they wear; withholding money or information; using physical size or status to intimidate; pressuring someone about consent.  
　解析：得分点拆分：每个具体例子 1 分，通常给 3–4 分。负面权力的共同点是「用地位、力量或信息差让对方失去选择权」——凡是让人不敢拒绝的做法都算，包括冷暴力和经济控制，不只是动手。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Negative use of power

**12.** Passive: holding back your own needs and going along with others, often to avoid conflict. Aggressive: putting your own needs first and disregarding or violating the other person's rights (shouting, blaming, threats). Passive-aggressive: expressing anger indirectly — sulking, sarcasm, silent treatment or gossiping instead of saying it directly. Assertive: stating your needs clearly and calmly while respecting the other person's needs, using 'I' statements and staying open to negotiation.  
　解析：得分点拆分：四种类型名称各 1 分（4 分），描述准确各 1 分（4 分）。判定关键是「自己的需求 vs 对方的需求」这个二维坐标：被动＝放弃自己；攻击＝压过对方；被动攻击＝不当面说、用冷暴力或阴阳怪气；果断＝两边都顾到。只写名称不描述，描述分全失。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Four communication styles（得分点按课程内容整理）

**13.** A sense of self is how a person sees and values themselves — their self-esteem, self-worth and personal identity. It includes their beliefs about who they are, their values, their strengths and weaknesses, and how they think others see them. It is shaped by family, peers, culture, media, school and personal experience, and it keeps changing through adolescence.  
　解析：得分点拆分：定义（怎么看自己）1–2 分；组成部分（自尊、自我价值、身份、价值观）1–2 分；影响因素（家庭、同伴、媒体、文化、经历）1 分。注意 sense of self 不等于「自信」——自信只是其中一个成分，只写自信只能拿部分分。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Sense of self（得分点按课程内容整理）

**14.** Stigma is a cluster of negative attitudes and beliefs that lead people to fear, reject, avoid and discriminate against those with a mental illness. It labels the person rather than the illness. For a young person it can cause shame and isolation, lead to bullying or being left out, and stop them telling anyone or seeking treatment, so the condition gets worse.  
　解析：得分点拆分：定义须包含「负面标签→恐惧／排斥／歧视」2 分；对青少年的影响 2 分（羞耻与孤立、被欺凌或排斥、不敢求助导致病情恶化）。只写「别人看不起他」这类口语表达只能拿 1 分，因为没有体现歧视与回避。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Stigma + Term 2 · Lesson 2 Myths, Misconceptions & Stigma

**15.** Depression: persistent low mood and loss of interest for weeks, with low confidence, changed appetite and sleep, tiredness and, at worst, suicidal thoughts. Anxiety: excessive worry with physical tension, difficulty concentrating, avoidance of situations and being easily startled. Bipolar disorder: mood swings between manic highs (elevated energy, little sleep, risky behaviour) and depressive lows. Schizophrenia: disturbance of thinking and perception, including delusions and hallucinations, which changes how the person interprets reality. (Eating disorders and PTSD are also acceptable answers.)  
　解析：得分点拆分：每种病名称 1 分＋典型症状描述 1 分，四组共 4–8 分。描述必须写该病特有的症状：把「心情不好」当抑郁、把「考试前紧张」当焦虑会失分。常见失分：四种都写了名称，但没有一个有具体症状。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Common mental health disorders + Term 2 · Lesson 1

**16.** Social changes: teenagers develop their own values and are influenced more by peers and media than by parents, so conflict over rules increases; they form a sexual identity and want more privacy; they seek independence and greater responsibility, and want to negotiate rather than be told what to do. Emotional changes: moods and feelings swing more widely, they become more sensitive to others' opinions and self-conscious about how they look, and they want to make their own decisions. As a result the relationship shifts from parent-controlled to more equal, with more negotiation and sometimes more conflict, and it usually settles again as independence is worked out.  
　解析：得分点拆分：社会变化至少 2 项并解释（2–3 分）；情感变化至少 2 项并解释（2–3 分）；指出关系总体从「被管」转向「协商／平等」并说明结果（1–2 分）。常见失分：只罗列清单里的关键词（values、identity、moods），不解释它们如何改变亲子互动，最多拿一半分。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Adolescent relationships

**17.** Gives eye contact and open body language; puts the phone away and stops other tasks; does not interrupt; lets the speaker finish before replying; asks clarifying questions; paraphrases or summarises what was said; shows empathy and does not judge; keeps the conversation confidential.  
　解析：得分点拆分：每个特征 1 分，通常给 4–5 分。要写可观察的具体行为（眼神接触、不打断、复述要点、把手机放下）；只写「认真听」「很关心他」这种抽象评价不给分。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Features of a good listener

**18.** Description: trust is the confidence that the other person will not hurt or betray you; honesty means telling the truth even when it is difficult; freedom is each person's right to their own friends, interests and choices; consent means a clear, freely given yes to any physical or emotional intimacy; open communication means saying what you think and feel, and listening in return; mutual respect means treating the other person as an equal whose needs matter. Interaction: without honesty there can be no trust, therefore a relationship built on small lies gradually loses its security; without open communication consent cannot be given or checked, so respect breaks down and conflict or harm follows. Implications: a relationship missing trust and respect tends to become controlling or collapse, leading to emotional harm, isolation and, for young people, damage to self-esteem and mental health.  
　解析：得分点拆分：描述 4 个行为各 1 分（4 分）；用连接词（therefore / as a result / leading to）说明行为之间的因果关系 2–3 分；讨论后果与影响（对个人、家庭、心理健康）2–3 分。分水岭在「分析」：只逐条解释、不写一个行为如何导致另一个行为，就拿不到分析与影响的分。  
　出处：Year 10 PDHPE Yearly Revision Questions 2023 · Analysing respectful relationships

**19.** A. Check the area is safe for you, bystanders and the casualty  
　解析：得分点拆分：顺序 1 分（D 在最前），理由 1 分（救援者自身安全优先）。D 是 Danger，不是 Defibrillation；现场不安全时应先撤离再呼救，而不是冲上去做 CPR。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 5 First Aid · DANGER

**20.** B. 30 compressions : 2 breaths  
　解析：得分点拆分：比例 30:2 记 1 分。还要知道开始 CPR 后只有三种情况可以停：救援到达、伤员恢复、或你体力上确实无法继续——这一条常在情景题里考。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 5 First Aid · CPR

**21.** Danger: stop safely with hazard lights on, keep clear of moving traffic and any fuel spill, and ask bystanders to warn oncoming vehicles. Response: check whether the rider responds by speaking to them and squeezing their shoulders. Send for help: call 000 or direct a bystander to do it. Airway: open the airway and clear any foreign material. Breathing: look, listen and feel; if breathing is not normal, start CPR at 30:2. Circulation: control any major bleeding. Defibrillation: apply an AED if available and follow the voice prompts.  
　解析：得分点拆分：D 描述现场危险与自保 2 分；R 检查反应 1 分；S 呼救并说明 000／指定他人 1 分；A、B、C 顺序与判断正确 3 分；D 取 AED 1 分；两项以上现场危险（来车、燃油、电线、玻璃碎片）1–2 分。顺序写错或漏掉 Send for help 会连锁失分。  
　出处：Term 1 · First Aid – Road Trauma + Term 3 · Lesson 5 First Aid · DRSABCD

**22.** C. About 33 m  
　解析：得分点拆分：先用 60 ÷ 3.6 = 16.7 m/s，再乘 2 秒约 33 m，1 分。最常见的错选是 17 m——那只是 1 秒的距离；题目给的是 2 秒。

**23.** D. Four times as likely
