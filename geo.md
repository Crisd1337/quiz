## 答案与解析

**1.** A. That environmental change operates at different rates and scales  
　解析：环境变化的时间尺度与空间尺度是 1.1 的核心概念：单次风暴可在数小时内改变海滩，沙丘迁移则需千年。两者并存，说明「变化」要按速率与尺度分别描述。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 1.1 Environmental Change（rate and scale of change）

**2.** B. Longshore drift, moving sediment progressively along the shore  
　解析：斜向入射的波浪使泥沙呈「之」字形沿岸移动，这就是沿岸流（longshore drift）。判读关键是 swash 斜向、backwash 垂直回落，两者合成沿岸净输沙。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 1.2 Coastal Processes and Environments

**3.** C. The build-up of sand so that the shoreline moves seaward  
　解析：accretion（淤积／增生）＝泥沙净增加、岸线向海推进；其反面是 erosion（侵蚀）。它与 dune formation 的关系是：先有沙源堆积，风再把沙向内陆搬运并被植被固定成沙丘。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 1.3 Changing Land Use and Implications on the Global Coastline

**4.** D. East-facing exposure, East Coast Lows and longshore sediment transport  
　解析：材料把脆弱性归因于：面向东侧的地理位置、暴露于东海岸低压、以及沿岸输沙。注意两个排名范围不同（NSW 第一、全澳第三），不要读反。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 2.1 Introduction to Collaroy–Narrabeen

**5.** A. An East Coast Low, bringing heavy rain and large erosion-driven waves  
　解析：密集等压线＝气压梯度大＝强风；闭合低压＋冷锋＝东海岸低压，带来暴雨、强风与大浪。这正是 2016 年该海滩遭受严重侵蚀的天气背景。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 2.2 Understanding East Coast Lows（synoptic map）

**6.** B. Housing was built inside the primary foredune zone  
　解析：对比历史分区图与现代航拍可看出：住宅已进入原前丘（primary foredune）范围。前丘本是天然缓冲带，被占用后风暴波可直接冲击建筑。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 2.3 Development in Collaroy–Narrabeen

**7.** C. Prevention — stopping change; mitigation — reducing impact; preparedness — planning response  
　解析：prevention 从源头避免（如禁止在前丘新建）、mitigation 减轻已发生变化的冲击（如海堤、补沙）、preparedness 为事件做准备（如预警与疏散）。分类决定评价标准，别混用。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lessons 3.1–3.2 Sustainable Management of Coastal Ecosystems

**8.** D. The wall reflects wave energy and removes sediment from the budget  
　解析：垂直海堤反射波能、冲刷堤脚，并打乱冲淤循环、改变沿岸输沙收支，导致堤前沙滩变窄、侵蚀向两端转移。这是 3.3 的核心机制，也是争议根源。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 3.3 The Controversial Seawall（Figure 1）

**9.** A. Succession, as wind and salt spray decline and the dune stabilises  
　解析：越向内陆，风与盐雾胁迫减弱、沙丘趋于稳定，植被密度上升——这是沙丘演替（succession）。样方沿样带布设的用途正是把这种梯度量化。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 4.2 Transects and Coastal Data

**10.** B. Less water and sediment reach the delta, so deposition falls and the coast erodes  
　解析：这是「沉积物饥饿」链条：上游筑坝引水→淡水与泥沙减少→沉积速率低于侵蚀／沉降→岸线后退、红树林退化。海平面上升会加剧，但不是唯一原因。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lessons 5.1–5.2 Introduction to Deltas / Management of the Indus Delta

**11.** Gradual change is slow and cumulative, such as long-term shoreline recession as sediment supply falls. Rapid change is triggered by a single event, such as the storm cut produced when an East Coast Low drives large waves against the beach.  
　解析：答题要点是「速率差异＋各举一例」。渐变例：长期岸线后退、沙丘演替；突变例：东海岸低压一夜之间造成的风暴侵蚀。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 1.1 Environmental Change

**12.** Waves approach the shore at an angle, so the swash carries sand diagonally up the beach, and the backwash returns it straight down the slope under gravity. Repeating this moves sediment progressively along the shore in one net direction.  
　解析：必须写出「斜向上升＋垂直回落」这一对动作，两者合成沿岸净输沙。只写「波浪把沙带走」不完整。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 1.2 Coastal Processes and Environments

**13.** Waves and currents deliver energy to the shore; erosion scours sediment from the beach face and dune during high-energy events; deposition rebuilds the beach and offshore bars when energy falls. The balance between erosion and deposition at a particular time determines whether the shoreline advances or retreats.  
　解析：答题结构是「能量输入→侵蚀→沉积→净结果」。关键是说明两者是动态平衡，而不是单向过程。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 1.2 Coastal Processes and Environments

**14.** Accretion is the net build-up of sand so that the shoreline moves seaward. Once sand accumulates above the reach of normal waves, wind transports it inland and vegetation traps it, building a foredune.  
　解析：链条是：泥沙净堆积（accretion）→ 露出常浪面之上 → 风搬运 → 植被拦截固定 → 前丘形成。缺一环就说不通。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 1.3 Changing Land Use and Implications on the Global Coastline

**15.** First, its east-facing aspect exposes it directly to East Coast Lows and the large waves and storm surge they generate. Second, longshore sediment transport moves sand along the shore, so the supply at any one point is limited and the beach has a narrow sediment store, leaving little buffer when a storm coincides with a high tide.  
　解析：自然因素要写「朝向暴露＋东海岸低压＋沿岸输沙／沙源有限」。人为因素（前丘被占）属另一题，这里答了不算错但不得分。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 2.1 Introduction to Collaroy–Narrabeen

**16.** Tightly packed isobars mean a steep pressure gradient and therefore strong winds, which generate large waves and a storm surge that raises water levels at the shore. The cold front adds intense rainfall and runoff, so the combination of higher water and bigger waves produced a major storm cut and foredune erosion.  
　解析：机制链：密集等压线→强风→大浪＋风暴增水；冷锋→暴雨。三者叠加正好赶上涨潮，才造成破坏性侵蚀。只写「风很大」拿不到满分。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 2.2 Understanding East Coast Lows

**17.** The foredune is the coastline's natural buffer: it absorbs wave energy and stores sand that can rebuild the beach after a storm. Removing it and building close to the shoreline means storm waves reach the structures directly, and the sediment store that would normally aid recovery has been lost, which accelerates long-term recession.  
　解析：要写出前丘的两项功能（消浪＋储沙），再说明两者同时丧失的后果。只说「离海太近」不足以解释脆弱性为何被放大。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 2.3 Development in Collaroy–Narrabeen

**18.** (a) Prevention — it removes the exposure before damage can occur. (b) Mitigation — it reduces the impact of wave energy on assets that already exist. (c) Preparedness — it plans the response for when an event happens.  
　解析：判断依据是「作用于变化之前／之中／之后」：预防在源头避免暴露，缓解降低既有资产的受损程度，准备安排事件发生时的应对。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lessons 3.1–3.2 Sustainable Management of Coastal Ecosystems

**19.** The protection argument is valid in the short term: the wall prevents wave attack on the houses immediately behind it. The criticism is also valid: a vertical wall reflects wave energy, removes sediment from the beach budget and can increase erosion at the ends of the wall, so the beach in front narrows and the problem shifts along the coast. The decision therefore trades protection of private assets against the loss of public beach.  
　解析：评估题要「两边都成立＋说明代价」：短期保护有效，但过程代价（反射、输沙收支改变、侵蚀转移）真实存在。只站一边不给高分。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 3.3 The Controversial Seawall（stakeholder perspectives）

**20.** A beach profile shows the shape of the beach — the position of the berm, the steepness of the beach face and the width of the beach — so the form can be compared between surveys. Raw data only gives distances and elevations as numbers, without showing how the surface is configured.  
　解析：剖面图的价值是「形态可视化＋可比性」：同一位置两次剖面可直接叠加比较；原始数据则要另行处理才能看出形状变化。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 4.2 Transects and Coastal Data（cross-section）

**21.** B is more reliable, because a larger sample across more sites is more representative and reduces the influence of a local anomaly, and using a consistent method makes the results comparable. A further improvement would be to repeat the survey at the same stage of the tide, or after a storm, so that change over time can be measured rather than assumed.  
　解析：可靠性三要素：样本量、覆盖范围、方法一致。改进建议要针对「可比性」或「时间重复」，而不是空泛地说「多做几次」。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 4.3 Mapping and Evaluating Coastal Fieldwork（reliability）

**22.** A river carries sediment downstream until it reaches the sea, where its velocity falls sharply. Because competence falls with velocity, the sediment can no longer be carried and is deposited; repeated deposition builds a low, shifting plain of sediment at the river mouth — the delta.  
　解析：链条必须完整：搬运→流速下降→搬运能力下降→沉积→堆积成三角洲。漏掉「competence 随流速下降」这一步是常见失分点。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lesson 5.1 Introduction to Deltas and The Indus River Delta

**23.** They reduce the volume of freshwater and the load of sediment reaching the delta, so deposition falls below the rate of erosion and subsidence. The delta surface lowers, seawater intrudes into creeks and soils, and mangroves die back because the freshwater and sediment they depend on are withheld.  
　解析：要写出「上游截流→水沙双减→沉积＜侵蚀＋沉降→地面降低、海水入侵→红树林退化」的完整链条，并点明这是「沉积物饥饿」。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lessons 5.1–5.2（dams, barrages and water diversion）

**24.** Positives: restored mangroves attenuate wave energy, reinforce the soil with their roots and trap sediment, so they rebuild the delta surface as well as restoring habitat and carbon storage, and the approach is community-based and largely self-maintaining once established. Limitations: mangroves cannot replace the water and sediment cut off upstream, so unless river flows are restored the gains are slow and limited in scale.  
　解析：评估要「正面机制（消浪、固土、拦沙）＋局限（无法替代上游水沙）」。只写好处是描述，不是评估。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lessons 5.2–5.3（Delta Blue Carbon Project）

**25.** The Indus Delta is more degraded. Its sediment supply has been cut at the source by upstream dams and diversion, so deposition no longer keeps pace with erosion and subsidence, and whole areas of mangrove have been lost — the damage affects the functioning of the entire delta, not just one shoreline. Collaroy–Narrabeen is heavily modified, but sediment is still delivered by longshore transport and the impacts are concentrated along a single developed beach, where the response has been local engineering plus planning controls.  
　解析：判据是「是否切断了系统赖以运转的物源」：印度河被切断水沙来源，属系统性退化；Collaroy 属局部改造过度。必须用表中证据，不能凭印象。  
　出处：Term 3 · files/Revision Activities.docx（Collaroy–Narrabeen vs Indus Delta 对比框架）＋ Lessons 2.1–2.3、5.1–5.3

**26.** At Collaroy–Narrabeen, hard engineering protects property in the short term but reflects wave energy, narrows the beach and shifts erosion along the coast, and it is expensive to maintain — so it is effective for private assets but weak for the public beach. In the Indus Delta, mangrove restoration works with natural processes, is low cost and largely self-maintaining, and restores habitat and carbon storage as well as protection, but it cannot restore the water and sediment cut off upstream, so it depends on river flows being reinstated. Judged against sustainability, the delta approach is more sustainable because it rebuilds natural capital rather than replacing it, which is why the seawall is better classified as mitigation than as a solution.  
　解析：8 分题要「两地各自评估＋明确判断＋理由」。判断标准落在「是否依靠并恢复自然过程」，并指出印度河方案的先决条件（恢复水沙），才算完整。  
　出处：Term 3 · Lessons 3.1–3.3、5.2–5.3；Assessment Notification — Structured Response（20 分 structured response）
