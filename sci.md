## 答案与解析

**1.** A. Current – amperes (A); voltage – volts (V); resistance – ohms (Ω)  
　解析：电流单位安培 A，电压单位伏特 V，电阻单位欧姆 Ω。在 V=IR 里 I 是电流、V 是电压、R 是电阻，符号不能互换。  
　出处：Term 4 · Yearly Examination（Section 1）+ 2020 Year 10 Yearly Examination Answers（Part C Q6 单位表）

**2.** B. The current is the same at every point in the circuit.  
　解析：串联只有一条通路，所以电流处处相同；电压由各元件分掉。只有并联才是各支路电压等于电源电压、总电阻更小。  
　出处：Electromagnetism.pdf（Series vs Parallel Circuits）

**3.** C. It glows with the same brightness as before.  
　解析：并联时每条支路都单独接在电源两端，电压都等于电源电压；一条支路断了，另一条支路的电压和电流不变，亮度不变。  
　出处：2020 Year 10 Yearly Examination Answers（Part C Q3 并联灯泡烧坏）

**4.** D. Globe 1 goes out and globe 2 becomes brighter than before.  
　解析：导线把 1 号灯短路，电流走电阻几乎为零的导线，1 号灯不亮；回路总电阻下降、电流增大，2 号灯更亮。短路≠断路。  
　出处：Electromagnetism.pdf（Series circuit 单一通路 / 电阻与电流关系）+ 课程标准补充（短路）

**5.** • Static electricity is the build-up of charge on an object, caused by the separation and accumulation of charged particles (e.g. rubbing a plastic comb through hair); the charges do not flow.
• Current electricity is the flow of charged particles (usually electrons) through a conductor such as copper.  
　解析：静电是电荷在物体上积聚、不流动；电流是电荷（通常是电子）在导体中持续流动。  
　出处：Electromagnetism Revision - Circuits（Lesson 1 Activity 2：What is Electricity?）

**6.** • Ohm's law: the voltage (potential difference) across a conductor equals the current through it multiplied by its resistance, V = IR.
• Units: volts (V) for voltage, amperes (A) for current, ohms (Ω) for resistance.  
　解析：欧姆定律 V=IR；单位 V、A、Ω。电流的符号是 I，不要和电压 V 混。  
　出处：Electromagnetism Revision - Ohm's Law（Lesson 2 Activity 2）+ STUDENT 7. Electromagnetism 2022.docx（Practical 2）

**7.** • Resistance is a measure of how much a material opposes (reduces) the flow of electric current through it.
• Resistance increases as the wire becomes longer and as the wire becomes thinner (smaller cross-sectional area); heating the wire also increases its resistance.  
　解析：电阻表示材料对电流的阻碍程度；导线越长、越细（横截面积越小）电阻越大，温度升高电阻也增大。  
　出处：Electromagnetism Revision - Ohm's Law（Lesson 1 Activity 4 Resistance）

**8.** • Current is measured with an ammeter, which is connected in series with the component.
• Voltage (potential difference) is measured with a voltmeter, which is connected in parallel across the component.  
　解析：电流表串联、电压表并联。名称、测量对象、连接方式三样都要写全才拿满分。  
　出处：STUDENT 7. Electromagnetism 2022.docx（Practical 1）+ Electromagnetism.pdf（Knowledge 2）

**9.** • Components in series carry the same current, so an ammeter in series measures the current that actually flows through the bulb.
• Components in parallel have the same potential difference across them, so a voltmeter in parallel measures the voltage across the bulb.
• A voltmeter has a very high resistance, so if it were placed in series it would reduce the current in the circuit and change the quantity being measured.  
　解析：串联元件电流相同，所以电流表要串；并联元件电压相同，所以电压表要并。电压表内阻很大，串进去会改变电路电流。  
　出处：Electromagnetism.pdf（Explain the difference between the positioning of the ammeter and the voltmeter）

• = 1.14 A (accept about 1.1 A)  
　解析：I=V/R=12÷10.5=1.14 A。先写公式再代数，单位不要漏。  
　出处：Electromagnetism Revision - Ohm's Law（Activity 3, Q2）

**11.** • R = V ÷ I = 240 V ÷ 0.25 A

**12.** • R = V ÷ I = 12 V ÷ 5 A = 2.4 Ω
• If the resistance falls below 2.4 Ω, the current rises above 5 A.
• The circuit breaker then trips (opens the circuit) and cuts off the current, protecting the equipment.  
　解析：R=12/5=2.4 Ω。电阻一旦低于 2.4 Ω，电流就超过 5 A，断路器跳闸切断电路——这就是它的保护作用。  
　出处：Electromagnetism Revision - Ohm's Law（Activity 3, Q4）

**13.** • (a) Series has only one path for the current; parallel has two or more paths.
• (b) In series the current is the same at every point; in parallel the current splits between the branches, so the total current is larger.
• (c) In series the supply voltage is shared between the components; in parallel each branch has the full supply voltage across it.
• (d) For the same resistors, the series arrangement has the larger total resistance, because the current must pass through every resistor one after another; in parallel the extra paths lower the total resistance.  
　解析：串联：一条路、电流处处相同、电压被分掉、总电阻更大；并联：多条路、电流分流、各支路电压=电源电压、总电阻更小。  
　出处：Electromagnetism.pdf（Series vs Parallel Circuits / Parallel Circuit）

**14.** • Each light gets the full supply voltage, so it glows at its normal brightness.
• If one globe fails the others keep working, because each branch is a separate path.
• Each light can be switched on or off independently — the same reason the classroom light switches are wired in parallel.  
　解析：并联各支路电压都等于电源电压、一个坏了不影响其他、还能单独开关——所以家庭和教室照明都用并联。  
　出处：Electromagnetism.pdf（Benefits of parallel circuits）+ Electromagnetism Revision - Circuits（Lesson 3 Q5）

**15.** • Current flows through the coil, creating a magnetic field around the current-carrying wire (Oersted's discovery).
• This field interacts with the field of the permanent magnet, producing a force (torque) on the coil, so the coil turns.
• The commutator and brushes reverse the current every half turn, so the coil keeps rotating in the same direction.  
　解析：通电线圈产生磁场，与永久磁铁磁场相互作用产生力矩使线圈转动；换向器每半圈换向，保证持续同向旋转。  
　出处：Term 3 Electromagnetism Week 10 Answers（Activity 3 · 电机转动原理）

**16.** • (a) Electromagnetic induction — a changing magnetic field induces a current in a conductor (Faraday's law).
• (b) Any two of: push the magnet in faster, use a stronger magnet, use more turns of wire, or use a coil with a larger area.  
　解析：磁铁与线圈相对运动→磁场变化→感应电流（法拉第电磁感应）。更快、更强磁铁、更多匝数、更大面积都能增大感应电流。  
　出处：Term 4 Electromagnetism Week 1 Answers（Faraday 电磁感应 / Activity 5 结论）

**17.** • (a) For both conductors the current increases as the voltage increases.
• The wire is a straight line through the origin (proportional), so its resistance is constant — it is ohmic.
• The light bulb curves: the current rises more and more slowly as voltage increases, so its resistance is increasing — it is non-ohmic.
• (b) gradient = ΔV/ΔI = (12 − 2) V ÷ (6.1 − 0.8) A ≈ 1.9 Ω, which represents the resistance of the wire, because R = V/I.  
　解析：两列电流都随电压增大；导线是过原点的直线（欧姆元件，电阻恒定），灯泡是曲线（非欧姆，电阻随温度升高）。梯度 ΔV/ΔI≈1.9 Ω 就是导线电阻。  
　出处：Electromagnetism.pdf（Voltage/Current 数据表）+ Electromagnetism Revision - Ohm's Law（Lesson 3, Q1、Q5）

**18.** • As the current increases, the tungsten filament heats up.
• The atoms in the hot filament vibrate more, so electrons collide with them more often and it becomes harder for charge to flow — resistance rises as the bulb gets hotter, so current is no longer proportional to voltage.
• The wire stays at almost constant temperature, so its resistance stays constant and V is proportional to I.  
　解析：灯丝通电后升温，原子振动加剧、电子碰撞增多，电阻随温度升高而增大，所以 V–I 图是曲线；导线温度几乎不变，电阻恒定，近似欧姆。  
　出处：Electromagnetism.pdf（Practical 2 Response：non-ohmic resistor）+ Electromagnetism Revision - Ohm's Law（Lesson 3, Q6）

**23.** • 1 Variation in a population — individuals differ in their inherited traits.
• 2 Selection pressure — an external agent such as a predator or a change in climate acts on the population.
• 3 Survival of the fittest — individuals whose traits suit the environment are more likely to survive.
• 4 Reproduction and inheritance — survivors breed and pass the helpful alleles on to their offspring.

**34.** • The ability to keep producing lactase is an inherited mutation, so there is heritable variation in the trait.
