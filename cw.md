## 答案与解析

**1.** A. 000 (ambulance, police or fire)  
　解析：000 是澳洲的紧急电话，救护车、警察、消防都是这个号。有人处于即时危险时必须打 000；其余三个都是心理支持与倾听热线，处理不了需要救护车的现场。  
　出处：Urgent Help（Call 000 — ambulance, police or fire services）

**2.** B. Lifeline on 13 11 14  
　解析：Lifeline 13 11 14 提供 24/7 的免费保密危机支持。校内的辅导员、SSO 和 Year Advisor 只在上班日的工作时间可用，所以半夜应先打热线。  
　出处：Urgent Help（Lifeline — 13 11 14, available 24/7）· Support people at school

**3.** C. 8–10 hours  
　解析：14–17 岁的建议量是 8–10 小时；7–11 小时属于「可能可以」的区间，少于 7 小时或超过 11 小时都属于不建议。睡眠不足会直接影响记忆、专注力和情绪。  
　出处：Sleep（Recommended sleep 表格：14–17 years — 8–10 hours）

**4.** D. two weeks  
　解析：关键判据是「持续超过两周」且已经影响日常生活。一天或几天的低落属于正常情绪波动，不等于抑郁症；而且材料强调只有医疗专业人员才能下诊断。  
　出处：Depression（lasting longer than two weeks and start to get in the way of your everyday life）

**5.** A. Inhale 4 – hold 4 – exhale 4 – hold 4  
　解析：箱式呼吸四个阶段各 4 秒，像走完一个正方形的四条边，重复至少 4 次。4-7-8 是同一页上的另一种技术，专门用于睡前脑子停不下来的时候，两者不要混。  
　出处：Anxiety（Breathing techniques：Box breathing 与 The 4-7-8 technique）

**6.** B. It is usually a way of coping with intense feelings, not attention seeking.  
　解析：材料明确写 self harm is not "attention seeking"：它通常是应对强烈情绪和压力的方式，也不等于有自杀念头。但它会带来长期的身心伤害，必须寻求专业帮助。  
　出处：Self Harm（self harm is not "attention seeking"；reasons people self harm）

**7.** C. Anger is the visible emotion, often hiding embarrassment, sadness or jealousy.  
　解析：冰山比喻的关键是：愤怒浮在水面之上，底下压着尴尬、挫败、难过、嫉妒等其他情绪。先找出真正被触发的情绪，才谈得上处理；材料也明确说压抑愤怒不是办法，学会管理才是。  
　出处：Anger（Why am I angry? — anger iceberg；external and internal events）

**8.** D. "Just cheer up and snap out of it."  
　解析：材料专门点名：不能对朋友说 cheer up、snap out of it、get over it，这会否定对方的感受。开放式提问、主动倾听、问「我能帮什么」才是有效回应。  
　出处：Depression（Take their feelings seriously — cannot just "cheer up"、"snap out of it"）

**9.** A. Fill in a referral form, a referral slip, or message a counsellor on Canvas.  
　解析：学校给了三条路：填 School Counsellor Referral Form、到 G201 对面辅导室填推荐条、或通过 Canvas 直接给辅导员发消息。见学校辅导员不需要医生转介（需要转介的是部分校外服务）。  
　出处：Support people at school（To organise an appointment with a school counsellor）

**10.** B. Work in 25-minute blocks with short breaks in between.  
　解析：番茄工作法是 25 分钟一段、中间短休。材料还补了一条硬规则：不管用什么节奏，都不要连续超过一小时不休息，因为拖太久效率反而下降。  
　出处：Study tips and tricks（Take breaks；Pomodoro technique — 25-minute intervals）

**11.** C. Checking your partner's phone and demanding to know where they are  
　解析：查手机、盘问行踪属于 controlling behaviour 加上 lack of trust，是材料列出的典型红旗。健康关系的四个要素是尊重、沟通、信任和支持。  
　出处：Relationships（Signs of an Unhealthy Relationship / What Makes a Healthy Relationship?）

**12.** D. Sex  
　解析：Sex 是生理性别（染色体、生殖器官等）；Gender 是认同与表达（男性、女性、非二元、性别流动、无性别等）；Sexuality 是被谁吸引（性取向）。Cisgender 指性别认同与出生指定性别一致。  
　出处：Identity and Gender（GENDER / SEX / SEXUALITY 对照表及 Key Terms）

**13.** Signs include becoming disengaged from activities he used to enjoy, significant behaviour or mood changes, relying on alcohol to relax, and changes in eating and sleeping habits. I would raise it with him calmly and suggest he talks to SSO Sara, who can refer him to a youth drug and alcohol service.  
　解析：材料把「脱离原本喜欢的活动」「情绪突变」「靠酒放松」「作息饮食紊乱」「耐受性上升」都列为 substance misuse 的信号；校内的第一站是 SSO Sara，可转介 DAYSS、SDECC 这类青少年戒治服务。  
　出处：Alcohol and Other Drugs（Signs of substance misuse or addiction；Where to go for support）

**14.** Encourage them to speak to a parent, a school counsellor or SSO Sara, and offer to go with them. If they cannot seek help themselves, ask their permission to tell an adult you trust — but if they refuse and you are still seriously concerned, tell a trusted adult anyway, and call 000 if they are injured or in immediate danger.  
　解析：安全优先于保密承诺。材料明确写：若对方不愿求助而你仍然非常担心，就应该去告诉值得信任的成年人；一旦已经受伤或有即时危险，立刻打 000。  
　出处：Depression（Help them find support）· Self Harm（Who can help?）· Urgent Help（call 000）

**15.** Box breathing (inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4, repeated at least four times) slows your breathing and calms your body's stress response. Planning a study timetable and studying a little every day spreads the workload and avoids last-minute cramming, which is itself a major source of stress.  
　解析：呼吸法处理「当下」的身体反应，时间管理处理「来源」，两者配合才有效。材料还提醒：连续学习超过一小时不休息，效率会下降，反而更焦虑。  
　出处：Anxiety（Box breathing；progressive muscle relaxation）· Study tips and tricks（Plan your time / Study every day / Take breaks）
