## 答案与解析

**1.** A. 000 (ambulance, police or fire)  
　Explanation: 000 is Australia's emergency number for ambulance, police, and fire services; it's for immediate danger, while the other three numbers are for psychological support and cannot handle emergency situations requiring an ambulance.  
　出处：Urgent Help（Call 000 — ambulance, police or fire services）

**2.** B. Lifeline on 13 11 14  
　Explanation: Lifeline 13 11 14 offers 24/7 free and confidential crisis support, whereas school counsellors, SSOs, and Year Advisors are only available during business hours, so call the hotline at night.  
　出处：Urgent Help（Lifeline — 13 11 14, available 24/7）· Support people at school

**3.** C. 8–10 hours  
　Explanation: The recommended sleep duration for 14-17 year olds is 8-10 hours, with 7-11 hours being 'possibly okay', and less than 7 hours or more than 11 hours being not recommended, as sleep deprivation affects memory, focus, and mood.  
　出处：Sleep (recommended sleep table: 14–17 years — 8–10 hours)

**4.** D. two weeks  
　Explanation: The key criterion is 'lasting more than two weeks' and affecting daily life, as one day or a few days of low mood is normal emotional fluctuation, not depression, and only medical professionals can diagnose.  
　出处：Depression（lasting longer than two weeks and start to get in the way of your everyday life）

**5.** A. Inhale 4 – hold 4 – exhale 4 – hold 4  
　Explanation: Box breathing has four stages, each lasting 4 seconds, like walking around a square, repeated at least 4 times, distinct from the 4-7-8 technique on the same page, used for sleep, and the two should not be confused.  
　出处：Anxiety (breathing techniques: box breathing and the 4-7-8 technique)

**6.** B. It is usually a way of coping with intense feelings, not attention seeking.  
　Explanation: Self-harm isn't about 'attention seeking': it's often a way to cope with intense emotions and stress, and it doesn't necessarily mean someone has suicidal thoughts, but it can lead to long-term physical and emotional harm, so professional help is necessary.  
　出处：Self Harm（self harm is not "attention seeking"；reasons people self harm）

**7.** C. Anger is the visible emotion, often hiding embarrassment, sadness or jealousy.  
　Explanation: The key to the iceberg analogy is that anger is visible on the surface, but underneath are emotions like embarrassment, frustration, sadness, and jealousy, so you need to identify the underlying emotions before you can deal with them; the material also clearly states that suppressing anger isn't the answer, learning to manage it is.  
　出处：Anger（Why am I angry? — anger iceberg；external and internal events）

**8.** D. "Just cheer up and snap out of it."  
　Explanation: The material specifically mentions that you shouldn't tell friends to 'cheer up', 'snap out of it', or 'get over it', as this dismisses their feelings; instead, use open-ended questions, active listening, and ask 'how can I help' for an effective response.  
　出处：Depression（Take their feelings seriously — cannot just "cheer up"、"snap out of it"）

**9.** A. Fill in a referral form, a referral slip, or message a counsellor on Canvas.  
　Explanation: The school provides three options: fill out the School Counsellor Referral Form, go to the counselling room opposite G201 to fill out a referral slip, or send a message to the counsellor through Canvas; seeing a school counsellor doesn't require a doctor's referral (some external services do).  
　出处：Support people at school（To organise an appointment with a school counsellor）

**10.** B. Work in 25-minute blocks with short breaks in between.  
　Explanation: The Pomodoro Technique involves 25-minute intervals with short breaks in between; the material also adds a hard rule: no matter what pace you use, don't work for more than an hour without taking a break, as productivity actually decreases if you drag on for too long.  
　出处：Study tips and tricks（Take breaks；Pomodoro technique — 25-minute intervals）

**11.** C. Checking your partner's phone and demanding to know where they are  
　Explanation: Checking someone's phone or questioning their whereabouts is controlling behaviour and a lack of trust, which are typical red flags listed in the material; the four elements of a healthy relationship are respect, communication, trust, and support.  
　出处：Relationships（Signs of an Unhealthy Relationship / What Makes a Healthy Relationship?）

**12.** D. Sex  
　Explanation: Sex refers to biological sex (chromosomes, reproductive organs, etc.), Gender refers to identity and expression (male, female, non-binary, gender fluid, etc.), and Sexuality refers to who you're attracted to; Cisgender means your gender identity matches your assigned sex at birth.  
　出处：Identity and Gender (GENDER / SEX / SEXUALITY comparison table and key terms)

**13.** Signs include becoming disengaged from activities he used to enjoy, significant behaviour or mood changes, relying on alcohol to relax, and changes in eating and sleeping habits. I would raise it with him calmly and suggest he talks to SSO Sara, who can refer him to a youth drug and alcohol service.  
　Explanation: The material lists 'avoiding activities you used to enjoy', 'mood swings', 'using alcohol to relax', 'disrupted sleep and diet', and 'increased tolerance' as signs of substance misuse; the first point of contact at school is SSO Sara, who can refer you to services like DAYSS and SDECC.  
　出处：Alcohol and Other Drugs（Signs of substance misuse or addiction；Where to go for support）

**14.** Encourage them to speak to a parent, a school counsellor or SSO Sara, and offer to go with them. If they cannot seek help themselves, ask their permission to tell an adult you trust — but if they refuse and you are still seriously concerned, tell a trusted adult anyway, and call 000 if they are injured or in immediate danger.  
　Explanation: Safety takes priority over confidentiality; the material clearly states that if someone doesn't want to seek help but you're still very concerned, you should tell a trusted adult; if someone is already hurt or in immediate danger, call 000 immediately.  
　出处：Depression（Help them find support）· Self Harm（Who can help?）· Urgent Help（call 000）

**15.** Box breathing (inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4, repeated at least four times) slows your breathing and calms your body's stress response. Planning a study timetable and studying a little every day spreads the workload and avoids last-minute cramming, which is itself a major source of stress.  
　Explanation: Breathing techniques help with the physical response 'in the moment', while time management helps with the source of the issue; the material also reminds us that studying for more than an hour without a break can decrease productivity and increase anxiety.  
　出处：Anxiety（Box breathing；progressive muscle relaxation）· Study tips and tricks（Plan your time / Study every day / Take breaks）
